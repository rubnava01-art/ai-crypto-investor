# Risk disclaimer

- Trading cryptocurrencies can result in the **loss of the entire capital**.
  Never trade with money you cannot afford to lose.
- **Stop-loss orders do not guarantee the execution price.** In fast markets,
  slippage can fill orders far from the stop level.
- Past performance — including backtests — does **not** predict future results.
  A good backtest does not mean a strategy is profitable.
- This software is provided for educational and research purposes. It is not
  financial advice.
- **Demo mode uses simulated data and simulated money.** Nothing in demo mode
  represents real funds, real orders, or real returns.
- Real trading (Phase 8) is disabled until: 30+ days of paper trading with
  net-positive expectancy after costs, acceptable drawdown, all tests green,
  and the operator's explicit written confirmation.
